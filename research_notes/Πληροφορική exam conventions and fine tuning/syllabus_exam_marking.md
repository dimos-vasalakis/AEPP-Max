# Πληροφορική (Γ' ΓΕΛ, Panhellenic exams): syllabus, paper structure and marking, 2026-27 (exam 2 June 2027)

Notes on method. The primary sources were read in full: the 2027 syllabus decision (ΥΑ 90176/Δ2, minedu PDF, text extracted locally), the ΙΕΠ teaching guidelines for 2026-27 (31-page PDF, mirrored on stadiodromia.gr), and the official exam papers of 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026 (PDFs mirrored by alfavita.gr, text extracted locally). Mark breakdowns per θέμα were added up from the papers' own "Μονάδες" lines.

## Q1. Official syllabus (ύλη) for 2026-27, textbooks, recent changes, ΓΛΩΣΣΑ and ψευδογλώσσα

### Takeaway
The 2027 exam syllabus is set by ΥΑ 90176/Δ2 of 6 July 2026 (signed by Σοφία Ζαχαράκη; no ΦΕΚ number appears in the published text). It covers two Diophantus textbooks and is unchanged from 2026. Answers must be written in ΓΛΩΣΣΑ unless the question asks for another representation from §2.3 of the main book (ψευδογλώσσα, flowchart and so on). ψευδογλώσσα is still examined when a question asks for it (2023 Β3 and 2025 Β2 did). No new textbook and no Python are in scope.

**ΙΕΠ teaching guidelines for 2026-27**
- They list five books. Only ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 1 and 2 define the examinable syllabus. The others are auxiliary: ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 3 «Οδηγίες Μελέτης Μαθητή» (Παράρτημα Α, 2η έκδοση), ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 4 «Τετράδιο Μαθητή» and ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 5 (indicative solutions for the supplementary book).
- Definitions must be taught only from ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 1 and 2. 150 teaching hours.

Source: [Οδηγίες διδασκαλίας 2026–27 (PDF mirror)](https://www.stadiodromia.gr/enhmer2sh/25_26/302.pdf); official listing on [ΙΕΠ](https://www.iep.edu.gr/yli-kai-odigies-didaskalias-genikou-lykeiou-gia-to-scholiko-etos-2026-2027/)

Syntax and scope rulings in the same guidelines that bear on marking (all from [Οδηγίες 2026–27](https://www.stadiodromia.gr/enhmer2sh/25_26/302.pdf)):
- **Quotes:** single or double quotation marks are both acceptable.
- **Repeat loop:** teach ΑΡΧΗ_ΕΠΑΝΑΛΗΨΗΣ…ΜΕΧΡΙΣ_ΟΤΟΥ (ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 1), but in exercise solutions also accept the Β' ΓΕΛ form Επανάλαβε…Μέχρις_ότου.
- **ΕΠΙΛΕΞΕ:** an additional valid syntax is declared, with an integer variable as the selector. The exact form is an image that did not extract. Convert ΑΝ…ΑΛΛΙΩΣ_ΑΝ…ΤΕΛΟΣ_ΑΝ to and from ΕΠΙΛΕΞΕ.
- **ΓΙΑ:** stress the ΒΗΜΑ (negative, positive, zero, omitted). With ΒΗΜΑ 0 the loop runs forever "σε κάθε περίπτωση".
- **Loop conversions** between the three loop structures are a separate teaching unit (3 h).
- **Expressions:** stress `div` vs `/`, operator precedence, and the precedence of comparison and logical operators; use parentheses.
- **Flowcharts:** students use the basic flowchart shapes, and simple flowcharts accompany every control-structure unit.
- **Linear search:** use a boolean flag ("done"/«σημαία») to avoid needless iterations and to never go out of the array's bounds.
- **Binary search:** taught as an exercise, iterative only (recursion is out of scope). The bound log2(n)+1 is not to be proved, and log2(n) must always be given.
- **Sorting:** the core method is straight exchange (bubble). Selection sort is taught as an exercise. Give special attention to sorting parallel arrays (names and marks).
- **2-D arrays:** max/min, search and sorting by row and by column; copying between 2-D and 1-D arrays. Only 2-D arrays are exercised; higher dimensions get just a mention.
- **Stack and queue:** static structures, implemented with 1-D arrays. Pushing and popping at the end of the array instead of the start is equally valid. Teaching the linear queue is enough, plus «ολίσθηση» (shifting) when the problem asks for it.
- **Functions** cannot contain input or output statements, so a function cannot call a procedure.
- **Black box:** simplified. One input and one output, and only the invalid values the problem states. Empty or type-incompatible input is excluded, because ΓΛΩΣΣΑ cannot handle it.
- **OOP:** simple ΓΛΩΣΣΑ-like examples may be shown, with the note that they would only be acceptable if ΓΛΩΣΣΑ had the extra features.

### Inferences
- For the tutor, the safest scope is exactly the decision's list. Anything in ch. 5, 11, 12 or 4.2+ of ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 1 (e.g. recursion, files, records) is out of scope and should not appear in solutions.
- "ΓΛΩΣΣΑ by default, ψευδογλώσσα or flowchart only when asked" is the governing rule. A solution in ψευδογλώσσα to a question that says «πρόγραμμα σε ΓΛΩΣΣΑ» risks losing marks, and vice versa.
- The tutor can rely on the syntax variants the guidelines explicitly accept (quotes, Επανάλαβε…Μέχρις_ότου, the extra ΕΠΙΛΕΞΕ form, stack at the end of the array). For anything else, stick to the textbook's forms.

## Q2. Structure of the exam paper (Θέματα Α–Δ, marks, question types)

### Takeaway
The paper has four θέματα of 25 marks each (100 in total) and lasts 3 hours. Α is theory (Σ/Λ, definitions, matching, short code). Β is short applied tasks (OOP class hierarchy, loop conversion, fill-in-the-blanks code, stack/queue operations, flowchart → ψευδογλώσσα, procedure ↔ function). Γ is a full ΓΛΩΣΣΑ program, usually sentinel-terminated with validation and statistics. Δ is a full program with 1-D and 2-D arrays, search and sorting, and a subprogram (usually a function). Every paper states that any scientifically justified answer is accepted.

### Cited Findings
**Official format**
- The exam has 4 θέματα containing theory and exercises/problems, of graded difficulty, 25 marks each. Θέμα 1 is theory only. Θέμα 2 is theory (15 marks) plus an exercise/problem. Θέματα 3 and 4 are exercises/problems.
- What each part assesses: theory questions test knowledge, understanding, critical ability, the use of theory to evaluate data and draw conclusions, and correct scientific terminology. Exercises test whether the candidate can combine knowledge and skills.
- Marks within a θέμα are split equally among its sub-questions unless the paper states otherwise.

Source: [CNN Greece 2023](https://www.cnn.gr/ellada/story/365051/panellinies-2023-pos-tha-vathmologithei-to-mathima-tis-pliroforikis). The ΥΑ/ΦΕΚ that defines this format was not cited there.

**Paper instructions (2025 and 2026)**
- 3 hours after the photocopies are handed out.
- Blue or black pen only. Pencil only if the question asks for it, and only for tables or diagrams.
- Notes written on the question sheets are not marked.
- «Κάθε απάντηση επιστημονικά τεκμηριωμένη είναι αποδεκτή.»

Source: [2026 paper](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-06/them_plirof_gel_260605_0_0.pdf); [2025 paper](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf)

**Exam dates:** 2023: Thu 8 June. 2024: Thu 6 June. 2025: Wed 4 June. 2026: Fri 5 June. — [2023](https://alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2023-06/%CE%A0%CE%9B%CE%97%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%97_%CE%9D%CE%95%CE%9F_%CE%97%CE%9C_2023_1.pdf), [2024](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2024-06/them_plirof_gel_240606.pdf), [2025](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf), [2026](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-06/them_plirof_gel_260605_0_0.pdf) papers

**Θέμα Α (25 marks)**
- **Α1:** always 5 Σωστό/Λάθος items for 10 marks.
- **Other parts:** definitions or short theory (4–6 marks each) and a matching item (5 marks).
- **Topics seen 2023–2026:**
  - converting loops;
  - FIFO and the queue;
  - "every function returns one value";
  - MOD on reals;
  - list insertion;
  - filling in a binary search tree;
  - basic operations on linked lists;
  - the criteria an algorithm must meet;
  - compiler and object program;
  - binary tree subtrees;
  - encapsulation;
  - push at the top of the stack;
  - superclass and subclass;
  - matching error categories (syntax / runtime / logic);
  - typical array processing;
  - graph definition and graph types;
  - "Διαίρει και Βασίλευε" and binary search;
  - abnormal termination when a letter is read into an integer;
  - interpreter vs object program;
  - whether `X ← 'α' < 'β'` is valid;
  - precedence of logical vs arithmetic operators;
  - ΓΛΩΣΣΑ code to push onto a stack in a 10-element array (5 marks);
  - arrays vs lists;
  - kinds of scope, and which one ΓΛΩΣΣΑ uses;
  - data vs information;
  - the runtime stack holding parameters;
  - the flowchart as a way to describe an algorithm;
  - iterations of `ΓΙΑ i ΑΠΟ 1 ΜΕΧΡΙ 5 ΜΕ_ΒΗΜΑ 5`;
  - black-box testing applied to subprograms;
  - singly linked list, NULL and head;
  - definition of a data structure;
  - matching operators (MOD, ΚΑΙ, <>, *, ^) to their types.

Sources: [2023](https://alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2023-06/%CE%A0%CE%9B%CE%97%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%97_%CE%9D%CE%95%CE%9F_%CE%97%CE%9C_2023_1.pdf), [2024](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2024-06/them_plirof_gel_240606.pdf), [2025](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf), [2026](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-06/them_plirof_gel_260605_0_0.pdf) papers

**Θέμα Β (25 marks)**, split 6/5/4/10 (2023, 2024), 9/9/7 (2025) and 10/5/10 (2026):
- **2023:** how many times ΓΡΑΨΕ runs in `ΓΙΑ i ΑΠΟ Α ΜΕΧΡΙ Μ ΜΕ_ΒΗΜΑ Β` for three sets of values; valid superclass–subclass pairs (ΝΑΙ/ΟΧΙ); stack/queue conditions (empty, full, one element, two elements) using top/front/rear, written «σε ψευδογλώσσα»; fill 5 blanks in a loop with two exit conditions.
- **2024:** convert nested ΓΙΑ loops (one with ΒΗΜΑ −1) to ΟΣΟ…ΕΠΑΝΑΛΑΒΕ; fill blanks to build a 4×4 array; front/rear after a series of queue operations; rewrite a ΔΙΑΔΙΚΑΣΙΑ as a ΣΥΝΑΡΤΗΣΗ and rewrite the calling code (7+3).
- **2025:** OOP class hierarchy with properties and methods; convert a flowchart **to ψευδογλώσσα** (9 marks); fill 7 blanks in a ΣΥΝΑΡΤΗΣΗ that checks a pattern in a 5×5 array.
- **2026:** OOP hierarchy diagram with a polymorphic method; convert ΑΡΧΗ_ΕΠΑΝΑΛΗΨΗΣ…ΜΕΧΡΙΣ_ΟΤΟΥ to ΟΣΟ…ΕΠΑΝΑΛΑΒΕ; fill 5 blanks in a bubble sort over a paired array (odd cells hold titles, even cells authors).

Sources: same four papers.

**Θέμα Γ (25 marks)**, a full ΓΛΩΣΣΑ program. Sub-questions:
- Γ1 is the declarations (2–4 marks).
- A read loop with a sentinel (name «ΤΕΛΟΣ», branch 0) or with multiple exit conditions.
- Input validation where the question asks for it.
- Selection logic.
- Max/min, often with ties or the top two.
- A percentage. Papers add a note such as "assume at least one customer", which removes the divide-by-zero case.
- 2023 also asked for a function (ΧΡΕΩΣΗ).

Sources: same four papers.

**Θέμα Δ (25 marks)**, a full program with arrays:
- Δ1 is the declarations plus reading a 1-D array of names and a 2-D array of data (often with validation).
- Row/column processing.
- Linear search with a "not found" message.
- Bubble sort of parallel arrays in descending order (with alphabetical tie-break in 2023; ties with the 10th student in 2025).
- A subprogram that takes the 2-D array and a row number (a function returning a sum or average in 2025 and 2026; a search subprogram returning the position or 0 in 2024).

Sources: same four papers.

**Per-part marks:** sub-questions often state marks per feature. Examples: 2026 Γ3 (α 2, β 2, γ 1) and Δ1 (α 1, β 1, γ 2); 2025 Δ2 (α 1, β 1, γ 3). — [2026](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-06/them_plirof_gel_260605_0_0.pdf), [2025](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf) papers

**When validation is required:** only when the paper says so («ελέγχοντας ότι…», «έλεγχος εγκυρότητας»). Papers also say explicitly when it is not required (2024 Δ1β «δεν απαιτείται έλεγχος εγκυρότητας»; 2025 Δ2α «χωρίς έλεγχο εγκυρότητας»). — [2024](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2024-06/them_plirof_gel_240606.pdf), [2025](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf) papers

**Difficulty commentary**
- **2026 paper:** a tutoring centre teacher (Π. Κάππος, Διακρότημα) called it among the most demanding of recent years: Α1 Σ/Λ needed deep knowledge, Β2 had a trap in the conversion, Γ was harder, Δ was standard but long. — [alfavita 2026](https://www.alfavita.gr/panellinies/518578_panellinies-2026-oi-ektimiseis-kai-apantiseis-gia-ta-themata-tis-pliroforikis)
- **ΕΠΕ's view:** graded difficulty and broad coverage. Γ4 (isolating the first branch whose stock ran out within a general loop) separated top from average scripts. Δ was above average but nothing was unfamiliar. — [ΕΠΕ 12/6/2026](https://www.epe.org.gr/ola-ta-arthra/deltio-typoy-panellinies-exetaseis-2026-scholiasmos-thematon-gia-to-mathima-tis-pliroforikis/)

### Inferences
- In the four papers from 2023 to 2026 there is **no classic "fill in the πίνακας τιμών" trace question**. The trace-like items are iteration counts (2023 Β1), front/rear after a series of operations (2024 Β3) and blanks that require mental execution (2026 Β3). The tutor should still practise trace tables (they are taught, «βηματική εκτέλεση»), but should not present them as a guaranteed exam item.
- Θέμα Β now reliably includes one OOP diagram item (2025, 2026) and one transformation item: a loop conversion (2024, 2026), a flowchart → ψευδογλώσσα (2025) or a procedure ↔ function (2024).
- Θέμα Δ almost always needs: parallel arrays (names + 2-D data), a subprogram that takes the whole array and a row index, linear search with a flag, and bubble sort that swaps the parallel arrays together.
- The CNN description says "Θέμα 2 = theory 15 + exercise". Recent Θέμα Β items are mostly applied rather than pure theory, so this split is nominal. A tutor should treat Β as short exercises.

### Gaps
- The legal act (ΥΑ/ΦΕΚ) that fixes the 4×25 format was not located. CNN 2023 paraphrases it without citing it.
- Papers before 2023 were not examined. Whether trace-table questions were frequent in 2020–2022, or in the ΑΕΠΠ era, is not verified here. A secondary claim that ΑΕΠΠ θέματα were once weighted 40/20/20/20 came from a site that now serves unrelated content and could not be verified.

## Q3. Official marking guidance (ΚΕΕ): alternative solutions, syntax slips, what costs marks

### Takeaway
No public, subject-specific ΚΕΕ marking scheme for Πληροφορική was found. What is official and public:
1. Every paper says any scientifically justified answer is accepted.
2. The ΚΕΕ's long-standing stance is that justified alternatives are accepted; it sends indicative answers and clarifications to grading centres only, not to the public.
3. The marks printed per sub-question (and sometimes per feature) define the rubric.
4. Two independent graders mark on a 0–100 scale; a gap of more than 12 sends the script to a third grader.

Specific deductions (missing declarations, syntax slips, wrong loop) are not publicly codified. The tutor should infer them from the per-feature marks.

### Cited Findings
**Alternative solutions and indicative answers**
- The paper's own instruction (2025 and 2026): «Κάθε απάντηση επιστημονικά τεκμηριωμένη είναι αποδεκτή». — [2026 paper](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-06/them_plirof_gel_260605_0_0.pdf); [2025 paper](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf)
- The ΚΕΕ historically did not publish indicative answers or grading guidelines, on the principle that "every justified answer is acceptable". It sends clarifications and instructions to grading centres so grading runs smoothly. (esos, 16/04/2018; older, but consistent with the 2026 paper wording.) — [esos 2018](https://www.esos.gr/arthra/56385/i-kee-de-dinei-endeiktikes-apantiseis-stis-panelladikes)
- In practice, ΚΕΕ indicative answers reach the grading centres and are "the basic reference frame" for graders. esos has published them for some subjects (e.g. Μαθηματικά 2026). — [esos 2026](https://www.esos.gr/arthra/99444/apokleistika-sto-esos-oi-endeiktikes-apantiseis-tis-kee-sta-mathimatika-poy)
- For Πληροφορική 2026, ΕΠΕ quotes the "ministry's indicative solutions" for Β3: 3 for gap (1) and 2 for gap (2). ΕΠΕ argues that other values also work (any value in (1,3] for gap 1, and 1 for gap 2, with redundant steps). It reports that the item was first treated as **closed-type** (no grader discretion) and later reclassified as not closed-type. This is direct evidence that the ΚΕΕ issues item-level guidance and adjusts it. — [ΕΠΕ 12/6/2026](https://www.epe.org.gr/ola-ta-arthra/deltio-typoy-panellinies-exetaseis-2026-scholiasmos-thematon-gia-to-mathima-tis-pliroforikis/)
- In other subjects, the ΚΕΕ has issued explicit instructions not to deduct marks for answers that differ from the indicative solutions but are scientifically sound and supported by the textbook (Μαθηματικά and Βιολογία, 2025). — [ethnos 2025](https://www.ethnos.gr/paideia/article/368721/panelladikesexetaseis2025odhgiesstoyskathhghtesapothnkentrikhepitrophgiathbathmologhshsemathhmatikakaibiologia); [esos, Βιολογία](https://www.esos.gr/arthra/88536/panelladikes-i-odigia-pros-toys-bathmologites-sto-mathima-tis-biologias)

**Grading process**
- Two independent graders, each marking 0–100 with grades hidden from each other. If the gap is 12 or less, the final grade is the average. If the gap is more than 12, a third grader marks without seeing the earlier grades. — [CNN Greece 28/5/2026](https://www.cnn.gr/ellada/story/535359/panellinies-2026-pos-diorthonontai-ta-grapta-pote-paremvainei-tritos-vathmologitis)
- With a third grader, the final grade comes from the two highest marks. — [alfavita](https://www.alfavita.gr/ekpaideysi/515473_pos-bathmologoyntai-ta-grapta-ton-panellinion-paraskinio-ton-diorthoseon-kai-i) (seen only in a search snippet). The CNN article's wording on this point is garbled.

**What the official texts say about syntax and form**
- Only the syllabus decision and the ΙΕΠ guidelines speak to this:
  - Solutions must be in ΓΛΩΣΣΑ "as defined and used in the books", unless the question asks for another form.
  - Some variants are explicitly accepted: single or double quotes, the Β' ΓΕΛ Επανάλαβε…Μέχρις_ότου, an extra ΕΠΙΛΕΞΕ form, and stack operations at either end.
  - A function must not contain input or output.

  — [ΥΑ 90176/Δ2](https://www.minedu.gov.gr/publications/90176_%CE%942_06_07_26_%CE%95%CE%9E%CE%95%CE%A4%CE%91%CE%A3%CE%A4%CE%95%CE%91_%CE%A5%CE%9B%CE%97_2027.pdf); [Οδηγίες 2026–27](https://www.stadiodromia.gr/enhmer2sh/25_26/302.pdf)
- Real ΓΛΩΣΣΑ cannot handle empty or type-incompatible input. Students are therefore not expected to validate against those, only against the invalid values the problem states. — [Οδηγίες 2026–27](https://www.stadiodromia.gr/enhmer2sh/25_26/302.pdf)

**General grading behaviour (not specific to Πληροφορική)**
- A small slip in an otherwise correct answer costs only that slip's share, while a conceptual error can cost the whole sub-question. For theory, the meaning matters and word-for-word recall is not required. — [ΑΟΘ Online](https://aothonline.gr/vathmologisi-stis-panelladikes-exetaseis/) (secondary, general)
- Graders penalise illegible answers and answers that leave reasoning implicit. — [ethnos 2023](https://www.ethnos.gr/paideia/article/261900/panellhnies2023tiprosexoynoibathmologhtesstagraptaposginetaihbathmologhshtasosgiatismegistesbathmologies) (secondary, general)

### Inferences
- **Alternatives are accepted.** A different but correct algorithm should get full marks: another loop structure, sorting method or search, or a function instead of inline code where the paper doesn't impose one. This follows from "κάθε απάντηση επιστημονικά τεκμηριωμένη". When a question names a structure ("χρησιμοποιώντας ΟΣΟ…ΕΠΑΝΑΛΑΒΕ", "να καλεί τη συνάρτηση ΜΕΣΟΣ", "πίνακας ΑΠΟΘ[150]"), not using it loses that part's marks.
- **Per-feature marks are the de facto rubric.** Declarations are a separately marked sub-question (1–4 marks), so missing or wrong declarations cost exactly those marks, and probably not more. Validation is marked separately (e.g. 2 of the 4 marks in 2026 Δ1γ), so skipping it when asked loses that part. Validating when not asked should cost nothing, but it adds no marks.
- **Closed-type items** (fill-the-blank, Σ/Λ, matching) are scored mechanically against the indicative answers unless the ΚΕΕ reclassifies them, as happened with 2026 Β3. Here the tutor should give the textbook-canonical answer, not merely an equivalent one.
- **Extra output:** printing wrong or unasked output is not addressed by any public source. Avoid it, and print exactly the messages the paper quotes.
- **Constructs outside the textbook** (e.g. Python syntax, `!=`, `==`, `+=`, recursion, globals, I/O inside a function) violate the "ΓΛΩΣΣΑ as defined in the books" rule. They should be treated as mark-losing.

### Gaps
- No public ΚΕΕ marking scheme or deduction table for Πληροφορική was found for any recent year. The ΚΕΕ's item-level instructions for 2023–2026 Πληροφορική were not found online; only ΕΠΕ's reference to the 2026 Β3 indicative values.
- The rules for "closed-type" items (who decides, and how grader disagreements on them are resolved) were not found in a primary source.
- Academic analyses of how ΑΕΠΠ answers are graded exist but were blocked (Cloudflare) and could not be read: a CETPE 2014 case study of the 2013 ΑΕΠΠ grading (Γώγουλος, Κοτσιφάκης, Παπαγιάννης, Χίνου), at https://eproceedings.epublishing.ekt.gr/index.php/cetpe/article/download/4378/4305/10937.

## Q4. Most common reasons students lose marks

### Takeaway
Greece publishes no official examiner reports for this subject, so this evidence is indirect. The recurring weak points are the ones the ΙΕΠ guidelines single out and that recent papers test or trap:
- ΓΙΑ with unusual ΒΗΜΑ values;
- loop conversions;
- array bounds and search flags;
- sorting parallel arrays;
- understanding (not memorising) bubble sort;
- isolating a "first occurrence" inside a loop;
- operator precedence and `div` vs `/`;
- subprogram rules;
- thin theory recall on the supplementary-book topics.

General causes are misreading the question, carelessness and running out of time on long Θέμα Δ parts.

### Cited Findings
**What the ΙΕΠ guidelines single out** — [Οδηγίες 2026–27](https://www.stadiodromia.gr/enhmer2sh/25_26/302.pdf)
- The ΓΙΑ step (negative, zero, omitted).
- Array bounds being exceeded in search; the flag variable as the remedy.
- `div` vs `/` and operator precedence.
- Comparison and logical precedence; using parentheses.
- Sorting parallel arrays.
- A function may contain no input or output.
- Black-box edge points when inputs are real numbers.

**What ΕΠΕ observed in 2026** — [ΕΠΕ 12/6/2026](https://www.epe.org.gr/ola-ta-arthra/deltio-typoy-panellinies-exetaseis-2026-scholiasmos-thematon-gia-to-mathima-tis-pliroforikis/)
- Students who had memorised bubble sort instead of understanding it struggled with Β3.
- Γ4 separated strong from average candidates.
- ΕΠΕ also states that debugging and black-box testing are barely taught in practice, and that OOP and dynamic structures can only be approached intuitively because the course has no language that supports them. This means those Θέμα Α and Β items are likely weak spots.

**Teacher commentary on 2026** — [alfavita 2026](https://www.alfavita.gr/panellinies/518578_panellinies-2026-oi-ektimiseis-kai-apantiseis-gia-ta-themata-tis-pliroforikis)
- A trap in Β2's loop conversion.
- Α1 Σ/Λ needed deep knowledge.
- Δ was long, so time pressure was a factor.

**Trap patterns in the papers themselves**
- A1 items on ΓΙΑ iteration counts with ΒΗΜΑ (`ΓΙΑ i ΑΠΟ 1 ΜΕΧΡΙ 5 ΜΕ_ΒΗΜΑ 5` runs once, not twice; 2026 answers mark item 4 as Λάθος). — [2026 paper](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-06/them_plirof_gel_260605_0_0.pdf); [2026 unofficial answers, Διακρότημα](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2026-06/%CE%91%CE%A0%CE%91%CE%9D%CE%A4%CE%97%CE%A3%CE%95%CE%99%CE%A3_%CE%A0%CE%9B%CE%97%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%97.pdf)
- MOD on a real (2023). — [2023 paper](https://alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2023-06/%CE%A0%CE%9B%CE%97%CE%A1%CE%9F%CE%A6%CE%9F%CE%A1%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%97_%CE%9D%CE%95%CE%9F_%CE%97%CE%9C_2023_1.pdf)
- Comparing characters, and logical vs arithmetic precedence (2025). — [2025 paper](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf)
- Ties in max/min and top-N (2024 Γ4, 2025 Δ4). — [2024](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2024-06/them_plirof_gel_240606.pdf), [2025](https://www.alfavita.gr/sites/default/files/2025-06/them_plirof_gel_250604.pdf) papers

**General causes** — [idietera, 23/3/2026](https://www.idietera.gr/articles/pliroforiki/pliroforiki-panellinion-ta-pio-syhna-lathi-kai-pos-na-ta-apofygeis) (general, written by a philologist); [csc.gr](https://csc.gr/articles/odigos-aepp-gia-tis-panellinies-pos-na-diavaseis-apodotika/) (tutoring centre; array start and end bounds named as a common error)
- Misreading the question.
- Carelessness.
- Weak reasoning.
- Too little practice on past papers.
- Poor time management.

### Inferences
Concrete checklist for an exam-grade solution (inferred from the rubric pattern and the guidelines, not from an official list):
- **Declarations:** write a complete ΜΕΤΑΒΛΗΤΕΣ (and ΣΤΑΘΕΡΕΣ) section with correct types and every array sized as the paper names it. A function's header declares its type, and its locals and parameters are declared inside it.
- **Validation:** validate exactly as asked, usually with ΑΡΧΗ_ΕΠΑΝΑΛΗΨΗΣ…ΜΕΧΡΙΣ_ΟΤΟΥ.
- **Sentinel loops:** read before the loop and again at its end, in the order the paper says (e.g. read the branch number, stop on 0, then read the quantity).
- **Initialise** counters and sums. Compute percentages with `/` and ×100, and rely on the paper's "assume at least one" note instead of adding a zero check.
- **Max/min and "first occurrence":** initialise from the first element, or use a flag; handle ties when asked.
- **Search** with a flag and a bounded loop (ΟΣΟ i ≤ N ΚΑΙ βρέθηκε = ΨΕΥΔΗΣ). Never read past the array's end.
- **Sorting:** straight-exchange (bubble) sort, swapping every parallel array together and respecting the stated order (descending, with alphabetical tie-break).
- **Subprograms:** call them exactly as specified, passing the whole array and a row index. A function has no ΔΙΑΒΑΣΕ/ΓΡΑΨΕ and assigns its name. A procedure is called with ΚΑΛΕΣΕ.
- **Output:** print the exact messages the paper quotes.

Theory answers (Θέμα Α) should use the textbook definitions (the ΙΕΠ says definitions come only from ΒΙΒΛΙΟ 1 and 2). The meaning matters more than exact wording.

### Gaps
- No official examiner report, ΙΕΠ/ΕΟΕ statistical analysis of answers, or teacher-association list of common Πληροφορική mistakes for 2023–2026 was found.
- A CETPE paper analysing graded ΑΕΠΠ scripts question by question exists but was blocked: https://eproceedings.epublishing.ekt.gr/index.php/cetpe/article/download/7872/6996/18602.
- Στέκι των Πληροφορικών (alkisg.mysch.gr/steki) grader discussions were not surfaced by search.
- The "common mistakes" above are therefore inferred from the ΙΕΠ emphases, ΕΠΕ commentary and the papers, and should be treated as informed inference, not documented statistics.
